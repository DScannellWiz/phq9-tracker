# Mental Health Tracker Closed Alpha Participation Acknowledgment v0.1

> **Historical and unused:** The formal Closed Alpha plan was abandoned before enrollment or distribution. Public users do not complete this acknowledgment or receive tester IDs.

## About This Closed Alpha

The Mental Health Tracker Closed Alpha runs from September 4 through September 18, 2026. Its only purpose is to evaluate and improve unfinished software, its distribution and instructions, usability, reliability, data integrity, workflows, and the usability of its generated outputs.

This is voluntary software testing for adults age 18 or older. You may stop participating at any time.

## Unfinished Software and Your Local Data

Mental Health Tracker is unfinished pre-release software. It may contain defects. Records, reports, exports, or other files could be lost, corrupted, duplicated, changed, or fail to work as expected.

The application stores the information you enter locally on your Windows computer. You control that local data and are responsible for making backups of anything you choose to preserve. Do not use this alpha as the only copy of information important to your health or care.

You may enter real, fictional, random, or mixed data at your discretion. The project will not ask which type you used. Whatever you enter remains yours and under your control.

## Not Medical, Clinical, Crisis, or Emergency Support

Mental Health Tracker and the Closed Alpha Program do not provide medical care, diagnosis, treatment, clinical monitoring, crisis support, emergency support, or guaranteed availability. The software does not alert Daniel, a clinician, a crisis service, or an emergency service.

Do not use the application or the support address for urgent, clinical, crisis, or emergency needs. Use appropriate professional, crisis, or emergency resources instead.

## Feedback Is About the Software Only

The project requests and accepts feedback about the software and supporting materials only. It does not request, collect, or analyze your medical or mental-health information or anything you enter into the tracker.

**Do not send assessment responses, journal entries, treatment information, clinician information, generated reports, Analysis Workbooks, databases, screenshots containing entered data, or any other medical or health information, whether real, fictional, random, mixed, or created solely for testing.**

This rule applies even if you believe the information is fictional, anonymous, or harmless. Describe the software feature, action, expected behavior, actual behavior, error message, and whether the issue can be reproduced without providing entered content.

If medical or health information is submitted, whether real or fictional, the project will not use, analyze, or intentionally retain it. It will be deleted or destroyed as soon as reasonably practicable after it is identified. The sender will be contacted only if necessary to request non-medical troubleshooting details.

## Tester ID, Feedback Records, and Contact Information

You will receive a tester ID such as `MHT-A001`. GitHub issues, findings, development records, and ordinary project records will use your tester ID rather than your name.

Daniel may know your identity and contact information so he can communicate with you and provide software or administrative support. A private identity/contact-to-ID roster will be kept separate from GitHub and ordinary project records and will be accessible only to Daniel. The target is to destroy that roster 30 days after alpha closeout unless a documented administrative need requires longer retention.

Pseudonymized feedback about the software may become a permanent part of the project's development and provenance records. It will not intentionally include your name, contact information, tracker-entered content, or medical or health information.

## Support and Feedback Period

`projectmentalhealthtracker@gmail.com` provides software and administrative support only. It is not continuously monitored and is never a clinical, crisis, or emergency support channel. Responses are best effort, generally targeted within 24-48 hours, but that timing is not guaranteed.

If you encounter a blocker or suspect data loss, stop the affected activity and contact the support address instead of repeatedly retrying it.

The active feedback and support cycle ends on September 18, 2026. You may retain and continue using the installed alpha afterward, or you may delete the application and your local data. After closeout, this build is no longer an active feedback or support version. Later feedback is not part of this alpha cycle, and fixes or support for an obsolete build are not promised.

## Compensation and Participation Choice

There is no financial or material compensation for participation. Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time and may retain or delete the application and your local data at closeout.
